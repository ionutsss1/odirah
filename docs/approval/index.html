URLs

Fragrances get their own top-level path (/fragrances/…) rather than sitting under a collection. If a scent is later moved or re-released in a limited edition, its link and search ranking stay intact.

Growth

A “Collections” menu appears in the navigation only when there are two or more collections. Until then, “The Archetypes” links straight to the one collection, so launch visitors never meet an index with a single entry.

IV

Motion & performance

The brief asks for refined animation that never gets in the way of buying. Every effect below has a still version for visitors who set their device to reduce motion, and none of them delays a price or a button from appearing.

Colour shift between archetypes

On the collection page, the background eases from one fragrance’s colour pairing to the next as each chapter takes over the screen, like light changing in a room.

CSS custom properties, scroll-driven, about 600 ms

Slow reveals

Illustrations open from a soft crop to full frame; manifesto lines fade in one after another. Text is always readable from the first frame.

IntersectionObserver · respects reduced motion

Page transitions

A short cross-fade between pages. A card’s image grows into the product page’s hero, so the visitor keeps their place.

View Transitions API, with a plain navigation fallback

Restraint

No parallax on mobile, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay sound. The hero film is muted, loops, and is replaced by a still image on slow connections or when data saver is on.

Save-Data and network hints

Performance budget

Largest content painted in under 2.5 s on a mid-range phone over 4G. Lighthouse mobile score of 90 or more on every template.

AVIF and WebP, responsive sizes, lazy loading below the fold

Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable drawer and menu, visible focus, alt text for every illustration, and only the colour pairings that pass contrast (see section V).

Tested with VoiceOver and keyboard only
V

Platform & content model

Shopify runs products, stock, payments, checkout and customer accounts. A custom Next.js front end draws every page, which gives full control over the storytelling and motion. Checkout stays on Shopify, which is secure, fast and familiar to shoppers.

VI

Open items & sign-off

None of these block approval of the structure. Each one is needed before or during the build, and is marked with when.

  • Discovery Kit vial size, 7 or 10 mlbefore build
  • Prices for each fragrance and the kit, and launch currenciesbefore build
  • Markets and languages at launch (shipping countries, EN only or more)before build
  • Web licences for Mrs Eaves and Sinfonietta, or the Sinfonietta names as SVG artworkbefore build
  • Wordmark and tagline lock-up as SVG filesbefore build
  • Hex values for the burgundy, olive and navy named in the manualbefore build
  • Narrative copy and top, heart and base notes for all sixduring build
  • Full-resolution illustrations, textures, campaign photography and hero filmduring build
  • Kit credit toward a full size: yes or noduring build
  • Customer accounts at launch, or guest checkout onlyduring build
  • Five to eight testers matching the target customer, for usability testingafter prototype
  • Legal texts: terms, privacy, cookies, returnsbefore launch

To approve

  1. The sitemap and URL structure
  2. The journey map and the two routes to checkout
  3. The screen layouts for desktop and mobile
  4. Shopify with a custom front end as the platform

What happens next

After approval we design high-fidelity screens for the homepage, collection and fragrance templates with real imagery, then build a clickable prototype on a Shopify development store with all seven products. Usability testing runs on that prototype before launch.
